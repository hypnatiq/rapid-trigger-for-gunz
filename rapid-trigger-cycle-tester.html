Rapid Trigger Cycle Tester

This tester follows one directional key through the two taps of one dash. The first tap is recognized, RT D controls how much you lift before the keyboard recognizes its release, and RT A controls how much you press again before the keyboard recognizes the second tap. This does not model an entire movement chain.

What this profile changes

Clan War / TDM

This is my current Wooting 60HE checkpoint for FreeStyle GunZ: 0.60 mm actuation, 0.15 mm RT A, and 0.25 mm RT D.

What may improve

  • 0.60 mm actuation reduces the first-press travel compared with my earlier 1.20 mm and 1.00 mm bases.
  • 0.15 mm RT A keeps the next press close to the movement rhythm I intend.
  • 0.25 mm RT D preserves more release margin than my earlier 0.15 mm checkpoint while still clearing the late dash.

What to watch

  • Shallow actuation can react to resting pressure or an unintended touch.
  • These values are my current personal checkpoint, not a universal preset.
  • A different switch, hand motion, client, or pace may need different values.
How I reached it: I preserved 1.20 mm and 1.00 mm as tested bases, then moved to 0.60 mm because it held up better across longer, higher-pace FreeStyle GunZ sessions. I paired it with 0.15 mm RT A and 0.25 mm RT D, changing one part of the cycle at a time.
